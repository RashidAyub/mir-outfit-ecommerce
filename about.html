The MIR OUTFIT Story

Designed for Modern Living, Tailored for Lasting Comfort

Founded with a singular commitment to craftsmanship, MIR OUTFIT blends contemporary streetwear aesthetics with classic bespoke tailoring. Every stitch, fold, and seam is carefully curated to bring you timeless pieces that transition seamlessly through every season.

We source the finest breathable cottons for our signature Summer line and heavyweight, insulating wools and puffers for our Winter collections. Our goal is simple: to make premium, runway-grade fashion accessible and durable for your daily wardrobe.

Our Guiding Principles

The core values that define every garment we create.

Uncompromising Quality

We never cut corners on raw materials. Each garment undergoes strict quality control to guarantee lasting color, strength, and softness.

All-Season Versatility

Whether it is lightweight summer casuals or insulated winter outerwear, our seasonal drops are tailored to your lifestyle and climate.

Ethical & Honest Pricing

Direct-to-consumer fashion without excessive retail markups. Premium wardrobe staples at fair and transparent prices.

Artisanal Precision

Mastery in Every Thread and Detail

From initial moodboard sketches to the final garment inspection, our production process honors modern craftsmanship standards.

  • Carefully Sourced Fibers: Breathable organic cotton blends, combed fleece, and durable denim.
  • Reinforced Seams: Double-needle topstitching for structural longevity through countless washes.
  • Modern Proportions: Precise tailored silhouettes designed for flattering, all-day confidence.
